SettingsHeader: 1rem of space under the header, and a gray border that shows once the panel's `SettingsBody` scrolls, so content no longer runs up under the title and description. The header's height does not change when the border appears. A header that puts its own content (a search input, column headers) in the default slot gets the same space and border; drop a bottom margin or border of your own there.
