DesktopShell: new `navSide` prop (`'left' | 'right'`, default `'left'`). On `'right'` the shell draws the navigation on the right and `SidebarRailItem` opens its tooltip toward the content and moves its active bar to the outer edge, and a collapsed `SidebarItem` opens its tooltip toward the content.
