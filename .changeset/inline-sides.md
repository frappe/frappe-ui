Popover, Tooltip, HoverCard, Dropdown, Select, Combobox, MultiSelect,
DatePicker, TimePicker and MultiEmailInput: `side` also takes `inline-start` and
`inline-end`, which open on the left in LTR and on the right on a `dir="rtl"`
page (and the other way round for `inline-end`).
